Möbius Kettle

Circulates heat through a short closed-time loop, bringing water to a boil seconds before the switch is pressed.

Built from
A thrift-store kettle, bicycle dynamo, copper induction coil, ceramic spacers, and a temporal phase ring.
Powered by
A mains heating element stabilized by the bicycle dynamo's phase-control circuit.
Side effect
A loop longer than four seconds can reheat the same water twice and trigger the thermal cutoff.
Utility
Surprisingly defensible

Prototype field notes

The first temporal coil overheated after 41 seconds, so I moved the return loop away from the kettle wall and added a ceramic spacer. Revision two reaches a boil before the switch is pressed, but only by 3.8 seconds. That is enough for tea, and not enough to upset causality.
